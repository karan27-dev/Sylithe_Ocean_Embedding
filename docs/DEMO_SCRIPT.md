| 0:20–1:11 | Closing card | Closing card | Before the prototype, the benchmark. Every method was scored on 2023, a year never seen in training, against 9,469 independent Argo measurements. Our model, in orange, beats the most recent published research, the Attention 3D U-Net++ of Wang et al. 2026, retrained on identical data, as well as ridge regression, climatology and the HYCOM operational model. Our three-model ensemble reaches 0.662 °C RMSE against GLORYS12, and 0.757 °C against Argo, the best of every competing model. Against that paper, our gain is statistically significant in the Bay of Bengal and in the deep ocean. Only GLORYS itself, which assimilates these same floats, scores lower against Argo. And the model is live. Visit sylithe-ocean-model.vercel.app. |
